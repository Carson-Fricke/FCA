# thesis/research

FCA hypotheses, investigation records and research models. Runtime records retain opaque namespaced links to this domain.

- `__init__.py`: Hypothesis artifacts, study models, and canonical research evidence.
- `evidence.py`: Pure parameter identities and numeric views of exact harness evidence.
- `grammar.py`: Shared value grammars for Thesis research artifacts.
- `hypothesis.py`: Falsifiable hypothesis artifacts as workspace Markdown documents.
- `investigations.py`: FCA investigation records and application-owned research persistence.
- `models.py`: Content-addressed statistical research models published per workspace.
